## Ano 'to

Ito yung sinasabi mo pag tinawag ka mag-report tapos wala kang na-prepare. Puro "very good" at "very big" lang, pero buong confidence.

## Saan galing

Galing sa Kimmy Dora, Kambal sa Kiyeme (2009), si Eugene Domingo ang nagsabi. Sa SPOT.ph ganito ang linya, "The business... is very good. And the office... is very big. And the bank... is very nice." Bagay daw 'to pag 'di ka handa sa presentation.

## Halimbawa

> recit kanina, the business is very good and the office is very big
>
> pano yung thesis defense mo? the bank is very nice
